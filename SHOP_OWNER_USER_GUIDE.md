## Getting Started

### 1. Creating Your Account
**Step-by-Step**:
1. Navigate to the BannerAI login page
2. Click **"Register"** if you're a new user
3. Fill in your details:
   - **Email**: Your business email
   - **Password**: Create a strong password (min. 8 characters)
   - **Full Name**: Your legal name
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. You'll receive a confirmation email - verify your email to activate your account
6. Log in with your credentials

### 2. First Login
- You'll land on your **Shop Owner Dashboard**
- The dashboard is empty initially (you haven't created shops yet)
- Start by clicking **"+ Create New Shop"** button

## Managing Your Shops

**Situation**: You own a retail business in Mumbai and want to add a second location in Bangalore.

**Steps**:
1. Click **"+ Create New Shop"** button
2. Fill in the shop details form:

   **Basic Information**:
   - Shop Name: `TechStore - Bangalore`
   - Description: `Electronics retail outlet in Bangalore`
   - Website: `https://techstore-bangalore.com` (optional)
   
   **Location Details**:
   - Country: Select "India"
   - State: Select "Karnataka"
   - District: Select "Bangalore"
   - City: `Bangalore`
   - Address: `123 MG Road, Bangalore`
   - Postal Code: `560001`
   - Phone: `+91-9876543210`
   - Email: `bangalore@techstore.com`

3. Click **"Save Shop"**
4. Your new shop is created with **"Active"** status
5. You'll see it appear in your Shops list immediately

**What You Can Do Next**:
- ✅ Create banners for this shop
- ✅ Assign team members to manage this shop
- ✅ Set up analytics tracking
- ✅ Configure shop-specific settings

### Scenario 3: Deactivating a Shop (Pause Operations)

**Situation**: You're temporarily closing your Bangalore shop for renovation (2 months).

**Steps**:
1. Find the shop in your list
2. Click **"More Options"** (three dots menu)
3. Select **"Deactivate Shop"**
4. Confirm the action
   - A popup asks: "Are you sure? Active banners will be paused."
   - Click **"Yes, Deactivate"**
5. Shop status changes to **"Inactive"** (🟡)

**What Happens**:
- ✅ All banners for this shop are paused
- ✅ Customers can't see these banners
- ✅ You can reactivate anytime by clicking "Activate"
- ✅ All data is preserved - no data loss

**When You're Ready to Reopen**:
1. Find the inactive shop
2. Click **"More Options"**
3. Select **"Activate Shop"**
4. Your banners become visible again instantly

### Scenario 4: Archiving a Shop (Permanent Closure)

**Situation**: You've decided to permanently close the Bangalore location.

**Steps**:
1. Find the shop in your list
2. Click **"More Options"** (three dots menu)
3. Select **"Archive Shop"**
4. Confirm: "Are you sure? This cannot be undone easily."
   - Click **"Yes, Archive Shop"**
5. Shop status changes to **"Archived"** (🔴)

**What Happens**:
- ✅ Shop is hidden from main list (moved to "Archived" section)
- ✅ All banners are permanently disabled
- ✅ Historical data and analytics are preserved for records
- ✅ Shop cannot be easily reactivated

**Note**: Contact support if you need to restore an archived shop.

### Scenario 3: Downgrading Your Subscription Plan

## Common Scenarios & Workflows

### Workflow 1: Adding a New Shop & Creating First Banner

**Timeline**: 10 minutes total

**Step 1: Create Shop** (3 minutes)
- Click **"+ Create Shop"**
- Fill in shop details
- Click **"Save"**

**Step 2: Create Banner** (5 minutes)
- Click **"Create Banner"** from shop detail page
- Design your banner (drag & drop components)
- Add images, text, colors
- Click **"Save Draft"**

**Step 3: Publish Banner** (2 minutes)
- Click **"Publish"**
- Set display schedule (always on / specific dates)
- Click **"Go Live"**
- Banner is now visible to customers!

**Result**: Your first banner is live and generating impressions within minutes!

---

### Workflow 2: Managing Multiple Shops Efficiently

**Scenario**: You have 5 shops and want to create similar banners for all.

**Option A: Manual Creation** (25 minutes)
1. Create banner for Shop 1
2. Publish it
3. Repeat for Shops 2-5

**Option B: Template Reuse** (10 minutes) ⭐ Faster!
1. Create a template banner
2. Click **"Save as Template"**
3. For each additional shop:
   - Go to shop
   - Click **"From Template"**
   - Select template
   - Click **"Create"** (customizes automatically)
   - Adjust colors/text for that shop
   - Publish

**Best Practice**: Use templates for consistent branding across all shops.

---

### Workflow 3: Seasonal Banner Campaign

**Scenario**: You want to run a "Festival Sale" banner across all shops for 2 weeks.

**Steps**:
1. Create the banner design
2. Add schedule:
   - Start: October 1, 2024
   - End: October 15, 2024
3. Clone/apply to all shops:
   - Shop 1: Mumbai
   - Shop 2: Bangalore
   - Shop 3: Delhi
   - (repeat for all)
4. Set different banners for different times:
   - 6 AM - 12 PM: "Morning Sale"
   - 12 PM - 6 PM: "Afternoon Deal"
   - 6 PM - 11 PM: "Evening Special"
5. Publish all
6. Monitor performance across all shops
7. After campaign ends, banners auto-unpublish

**Time Saved**: All shops running same campaign, but you managed from one place!

---

### Workflow 4: Responding to Low Performance

**Scenario**: One shop's banners have very low CTR (1%) compared to others (5%).

**Investigation Steps**:
1. Check **Shop Analytics**:
   - Is traffic low? → Problem might be visibility
   - Is traffic high but clicks low? → Problem is banner design

2. **A/B Test Solution**:
   - Create alternative banner design
   - Run both versions simultaneously
   - Compare CTR
   - Keep the winner

3. **Optimize Based on Data**:
   - Device data says: 80% mobile traffic → Optimize for mobile
   - Time data says: Peak at 8 PM → Schedule banners then
   - Device data says: Desktop performs better → Focus on desktop

4. **Implementation**:
   - Update banners with insights
   - Re-publish
   - Monitor for improvement
   - If CTR improves → You've fixed it!

## Troubleshooting

### Problem 1: Can't Create More Shops

**Symptom**: "Create New Shop" button is disabled
**Cause**: You've reached your plan's shop limit
**Solution**:
1. Go to **Subscription**
2. Upgrade to a plan with more shops
3. Come back - button is now enabled!

---

### Problem 2: Banner Not Showing on Banners List

**Symptom**: Created a banner but can't find it
**Cause**: Possible reasons:
- Banners are archived
- You're looking at wrong shop
- Banner is in draft status

**Solution**:
1. Check **Shop**: Make sure you're looking at right shop
2. Check **Status**: Click "Show Drafts" to see unpublished banners
3. Check **Archives**: Click "Show Archived" to see old banners

---

### Problem 3: Analytics Not Updating

**Symptom**: Analytics page shows old data
**Cause**: Data refreshes every 6 hours, not in real-time
**Solution**:
1. Wait 6 hours for latest data
2. Or click **"Refresh Data"** button (manual refresh)
3. Data will update within 5 minutes

---

### Problem 4: Subscription Payment Failed

**Symptom**: Email: "Payment failed. Subscription at risk."
**Cause**: Credit card declined or expired
**Solution**:
1. Go to **Subscription** → **"Manage Billing"**
2. Click **"Update Payment Method"**
3. Enter new credit card details
4. Click **"Save"**
5. Retry payment
6. Success! Subscription is active again

---

### Problem 5: Can't Deactivate Shop (Validation Error)

**Symptom**: Error: "Cannot deactivate. This shop has active contracts."
**Cause**: Shop has active service agreements
**Solution**:
1. Contact Support to terminate contracts
2. Or archive shop instead of deactivating
3. Or wait for contracts to expire naturally
