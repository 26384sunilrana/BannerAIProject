## Getting Started

### Welcome to Banner Editor! 👋

Banner Editor is an easy-to-use tool to create beautiful banners without coding. This guide will walk you through everything step-by-step.

### What You'll Learn
- ✅ Create a new banner
- ✅ Add text, images, and videos
- ✅ Design and customize
- ✅ Save your work
- ✅ Preview and publish

**Time to Complete**: ~30 minutes for your first banner

## Login & Access

### Step 1: Go to Login Page

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: `https://app.bannereditor.app`
3. You should see login screen

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

1. **Email**: Enter your email address
2. **Password**: Enter your password
3. Click **Login** button

### Step 3: Access Your Shop

1. If you have multiple shops, select your shop
2. Click **Enter** or **Continue**
3. You're now in your dashboard!

### Troubleshooting Login

**Forgot Password?**
1. Click **Forgot Password?** link
2. Enter your email
3. Click **Send Reset Link**
4. Check your email for reset link
5. Follow link to create new password

**Account Locked?**
1. After 5 wrong login attempts, account locks
2. Contact your shop owner to unlock
3. Or wait 24 hours for auto-unlock

## Create First Banner

### Step 1: Start New Banner

**Method 1: From Dashboard**
1. Click **+ Create New Banner** button
2. Go to Step 2

**Method 2: From Menu**
1. Click **Banners** in left sidebar
2. Click **Create New** button
3. Go to Step 2

### Step 2: Banner Settings

A form appears with these fields:

1. **Banner Name** (required)
   - Example: "Summer Sale 2026"
   - Choose descriptive name
   - You can change later

2. **Width** (default: 1200)
   - Recommended: 1200 pixels
   - You can adjust later

3. **Height** (default: 600)
   - Recommended: 600 pixels
   - You can adjust later

4. **Background Color**
   - Click color picker
   - Choose color from palette
   - Or enter hex code (#FFFFFF)
   - White is safest default

### Step 3: Create Banner

1. Review all settings
2. Click **Create Banner**
3. Wait for editor to load (5-10 seconds)
4. Banner editor opens with blank canvas

```
│ • Video                                •P│

└─────────────────────────────────────────
```

### Add Text

**Steps**:
1. Click **Text** in left toolbar
2. Move cursor to canvas
3. Click where you want text
4. Type your text
5. Text appears on banner

**Format Text**:
1. Text is automatically selected
2. Right panel shows options:
   - **Font**: Arial, Helvetica, Times, etc.
   - **Size**: 12-72 (larger = bigger text)
   - **Color**: Click to choose color
   - **Bold/Italic**: Click buttons

3. Make changes, see updates instantly

**Move & Resize Text**:
1. Click text to select
2. Drag text to move
3. Drag corner to resize
4. Or use arrow keys to move 1 pixel

**Example**: 
- Create heading: Font size 48, bold, black
- Create description: Font size 24, regular, gray

### Add Image

**Steps**:
1. Click **Image** in left toolbar
2. Click on canvas where you want image
3. Right panel shows upload button
4. Click **Upload** or **Choose File**
5. Select image from your computer
6. Image appears on banner

**Supported Formats**:
- JPG (.jpg, .jpeg)
- PNG (.png)
- WebP (.webp)
- GIF (.gif)

**File Size**: Maximum 50MB (usually unnecessary)

**Resize & Position Image**:
1. Click image to select
2. Drag corners to resize
3. Drag image to move
4. Try different positions

### Add Video

**Steps**:
1. Click **Video** in left toolbar
2. Click on canvas
3. Right panel shows **Upload**
4. Click **Upload** or **Choose File**
5. Select video from computer
6. Wait for upload (may take time)
7. Video appears on banner

**Supported Formats**:
- MP4 (.mp4)
- WebM (.webm)

**Video Settings**:
1. **Play Controls**: Show/hide buttons
2. **Auto-play**: Start automatically
3. **Loop**: Repeat video
4. **Mute**: No sound

**Tip**: Videos larger than 100MB may take time to upload. Use video compression tools first.

### Add Shapes

**Steps**:
1. Click **Shape** in left toolbar
2. Choose shape:
   - Rectangle (square or rounded)
   - Circle
   - Triangle
   - Line

3. Click on canvas to place
4. Drag corners to resize
5. Right panel to customize:
   - **Color**: Fill color
   - **Outline**: Border color
   - **Border Size**: Thickness
   - **Opacity**: Transparency (0-100%)

## Publish Banner

### Before Publishing Checklist

Before you publish, verify:
- [ ] Text is readable and correct
- [ ] Images are properly positioned
- [ ] No spelling mistakes
- [ ] All components visible
- [ ] Looks good in preview
- [ ] Looks good on mobile

### Publish Banner

**Steps**:
1. Click **Publish** button (top right)
2. Dialog appears asking:
   - "Make live now?" 
   - Or "Schedule for later?"

3. **Option A: Publish Now**
   - Click **Publish Now**
   - Confirmation message
   - Banner is now LIVE

4. **Option B: Schedule**
   - Click **Schedule**
   - Pick date and time
   - Click **Schedule**
   - Banner will auto-publish at that time

### Share Published Banner

**After publishing**:
1. You get a **shareable link**
2. Share link with others:
   - Via email
   - In messages
   - On social media
   - On your website

**Example Link**: `https://app.bannereditor.app/banner/xyz123`

### Unpublish Banner

**If you want to remove from public**:
1. Click **Unpublish** button
2. Confirm unpublishing
3. Banner no longer public
4. Saved as draft
5. Can republish later

## Tips for Beginners

### 🎨 Design Tips

**What Makes Good Banner?**
1. **Clear Message**: One main idea
2. **Readable Text**: Big, dark text on light background
3. **Good Image**: High quality, relevant photo
4. **Simple Design**: Don't overcrowd
5. **Call to Action**: Tell viewer what to do

**Color Tips**:
- Use 2-3 colors max
- Dark text on light background
- Bright color for buttons/CTAs
- Test contrast (can you read it?)

**Text Tips**:
- Keep text SHORT
- Headlines: 40+ font size
- Body text: 20+ font size
- Use simple fonts (Arial, Helvetica)

### ⏱️ Time Management

**Typical Timeline**:
- **Simple banner**: 15-30 minutes
- **Moderate banner**: 30-60 minutes
- **Complex banner**: 1-2 hours

**Pro Tip**: Start simple, add complexity gradually

### 📸 Image Tips

**Where to Find Images**:
- Take your own photos
- Stock photo sites:
  - Unsplash (free)
  - Pixabay (free)
  - Pexels (free)
  - Canva (paid)

**Image Guidelines**:
- High resolution (1920x1080 minimum)
- Relevant to content
- Face center subject
- Consider text overlay

### 🚀 Getting Help

**When You're Stuck**:
1. Click **Help** icon (? button)
2. Search for your issue
3. Watch video tutorial
4. Or contact support

## Troubleshooting

### Q: I can't see my text after adding it
**A**: 
1. Text might be white on white background
2. Check text color in right panel
3. Change to dark color (black or dark blue)
4. Text should now be visible

### Q: Image quality looks bad
**A**:
1. Original image may be low quality
2. Find higher resolution image (1920x1080+)
3. Or use image editor to enhance
4. Upload new image
5. Delete old one

### Q: Can't upload video
**A**:
1. Check file format (MP4 or WebM only)
2. Check file size (may take time for large files)
3. Check internet connection
4. Try uploading again
5. Or convert video first

### Q: How do I undo my changes?
**A**:
1. Click **Undo** button
2. Or press Ctrl + Z
3. Or Ctrl + Y to redo
4. Each action can be undone individually

### Q: I published by mistake
**A**:
1. Click **Unpublish** button
2. Banner removed from public
3. Saved as draft
4. You can edit again
5. Republish when ready

### Q: How do I delete my banner?
**A**:
1. Go to Banners list
2. Find your banner
3. Click menu (three dots)
4. Click **Delete**
5. Confirm deletion
6. Banner permanently deleted
